THE MATHEMATICS

SHA-256 schedule recurrence

W[t] = σ1(W[t-2]) + W[t-7]
     + σ0(W[t-15]) + W[t-16]

Reverse:

W[t-16] = W[t] - σ1(W[t-2])
        - W[t-7] - σ0(W[t-15])

All arithmetic is modulo 2^32.
σ0(x) = ROTR7(x) XOR ROTR18(x) XOR SHR3(x)
σ1(x) = ROTR17(x) XOR ROTR19(x) XOR SHR10(x)
