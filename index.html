SHA-256 Pattern Lab

Explore hashing, message schedules and mathematical patterns.

01 — Hash Analyser

Inspect a SHA-256 digest and its basic properties.

02 — Schedule Laboratory

Enter a message to calculate its SHA-256 hash and message schedule.

03 — Reverse Schedule Lab

Use the final 16 message-schedule words to calculate the earlier words.

This reverses the SHA-256 message-schedule expansion. It does not reverse the final SHA-256 digest into an original message.

04 — Pattern Explorer

Compare two messages and examine how their hashes and message schedules differ.

05 — Input Verifier

Check whether a message you supply matches a SHA-256 digest you supply. This is intended for your own educational test examples.
